## Agent OS

### 01. PROGRESSIVE TRUST SYSTEM

Agents earn autonomy through demonstrated reliability, progressing from observation to action. Every operation is isolated, auditable, and governed from the start.

### 02. ADAPTIVE SYSTEM GENERATION

Users describe intent in natural language, and Sycamore generates production-ready systems, including applications, integrations, and agents, tailored to the enterprise environment.

### 03. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

Agents learn from outcomes, improving performance over time while capturing institutional knowledge across deployments.

### 04. COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE

Sycamore surfaces organizational knowledge, connecting data, workflows, and expertise across teams to enable coordinated, multi-agent execution.

## Trust by design.

Enterprises need a trust and governance layer before autonomy can scale. Sycamore provides it with security, compliance, and auditability built in.

- **Secure**: Isolated and governed from the start
- **Compliant**: Roles, permissions, and control planes
- **Auditable**: Every action logged and traceable

## Labs

We research the hard problems standing between today’s AI and tomorrow’s autonomous enterprise. Our work spans agent infrastructure, safety, and learning systems, building the foundations that make reliable autonomy possible.

### Multi-Agent Orchestration & Reliability

Coordination protocols and fault-tolerance mechanisms for systems where multiple agents collaborate, delegate, and recover from failure, growing more reliable the more they’re used.

### Agent Safety & Security

Agents that stay within bounds, resist adversarial inputs, and behave predictably, even in environments they weren’t explicitly designed for.

### Long-Horizon Memory & Context

Maintaining coherent goals, recalling relevant history, and reasoning across extended interactions, not just the last few turns.

### Meta-Learning

How agents learn to learn: acquiring new skills, adapting to novel environments, and continuously improving without retraining from scratch.

### Progressive Trust & Governance

Frameworks for agents to earn autonomy through demonstrated reliability, with isolation, auditability, and governance built into every operation.

### Adaptive System Generation

Generating production-ready applications, integrations, and agents from natural language intent, tailored to each organization’s environment.
